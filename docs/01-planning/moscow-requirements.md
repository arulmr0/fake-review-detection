`priority:could`, so the board and this table cannot drift apart. The
traceability column names the issue that implements each requirement.

## Note on the Semester 1/2 boundary

C1, C2 and C4 were originally planned for Semester 1. The supervisor's
point in Week 8 was that a feasibility study does not need significance
testing or explainability, and the week was better spent on D1. They
moved to milestone M5 rather than being dropped.
