# Risk register

Opened Week 3, reviewed at every supervision meeting. Closing position
for Semester 1 is recorded below; R5 and R6 carry into Semester 2.

> The High/Medium/Low scores here are the working tracker. For the
> **quantified** version — probability, impact in project days, exposure,
> trigger and contingency path, plus R7 and R8 — see §5 of
> [`../02-research/feasibility-study.md`](../02-research/feasibility-study.md).
> Residual exposure on the open risks is ≈ 6.8 project days (≈ 27 hours),
> which is what the Semester 2 contingency buffer is sized against.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Access to real platform review data (Yelp) is restricted | M | M | Treat as Semester 2 scope; Semester 1 uses the two corpora already secured; Amazon Reviews 2023 as fallback | **Closed** by descoping (W10) |
| R2 | Insufficient compute to fine-tune a transformer | H | M | Free-tier GPU service, `distilroberta-base`, shorter sequences, smaller batch | **Closed** W7 — twelve-minute run on a T4 |
| R3 | Ethics approval not returned in time | L | H | Submit 28 Oct, two days early; no Semester 1 work depends on approval being returned | **Closed** W7 |
| R4 | D1 and D2 fall in the same week as other coursework | H | H | D1 draft to supervisor 12 Nov; video recorded by 20 Nov; Week 11 reserved for revision only | **Closed** W10 — both finished before W11 |
| R5 | Results are not comparable with published figures: the corpora differ in domain and in how the fake text was produced | M | M | Report within-dataset and cross-dataset separately; state dataset, split and seed in every table; limitations section says what the numbers cannot support | **Open**, managed |
| R6 | The Semester 2 user evaluation needs an ethics amendment the current approval does not cover | L | H | Draft over the winter break, submit in Week 1; design the evaluation so a pilot can run if approval is delayed | **Open** |

## Risks that materialised

**R4** was the one to watch and it did not bite, because the mitigation
was in place from Week 3. Other coursework did cluster in Weeks 8–10 as
predicted; the experimental work had already been front-loaded into
Weeks 4–7, leaving only writing, which slices into short sessions far
better than debugging does.

**R2** materialised immediately — `roberta-base` would not load on the
laptop in Week 4 — and was resolved in Week 7. This risk mattered more
than it first looked: D2 has to demonstrate that the Semester 2 plan is
achievable with the compute actually available, so an unresolved R2
would have undermined the video, not merely an experiment.

## Considered and not registered

- *Losing work.* GitHub is the primary copy, with a local clone and an
  offline copy on an external drive. Registering it would be theatre.
- *Supervisor unavailable.* The fortnightly slot was agreed in Week 3
  and email in between has been answered within a day throughout.
